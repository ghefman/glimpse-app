# Glimpse

**Your family photos, turned into a party game.** One phone hosts the game and can be mirrored to a TV. Everyone else joins from their own phone's browser with nothing to install and no accounts needed. The whole game runs on your Wi-Fi: no internet needed, no servers, no ads.

## How it plays

1. **Add photos** on the host phone: family trips, old albums, anything. 25 photos or more is best.
2. **Mirror the phone to the TV** (AirPlay screen mirroring), or open the TV address in the TV's browser.
3. **Everyone scans the QR code** on the TV and picks a nickname. No app needed, any phone browser works.
4. **The storyteller** secretly chooses one of 25 photos on their phone, says a single word about it out loud, and only then taps it.
5. **The others find it** among 6 photos on the TV and vote from their phones.
6. **Points**: if some players find it, they and the storyteller score. If everyone finds it, the word was too easy.
7. **The storyteller** role moves to the next player.

## What you need

- One phone or tablet running Glimpse (the host).
- 3 or more players, each with any phone with a web browser.
- Everyone on the same Wi-Fi, or (in iPhone only) on the host phone's Personal Hotspot.
- A TV is optional but more fun: screen mirroring, or the TV's own browser.

## Private by design

Your photos never leave your home network. The host phone runs the game itself, and the other phones talk only to it, over your Wi-Fi. The app has no online service behind it; it only checks for app updates when it has internet.

- No accounts or sign-up
- No data collection, analytics, or tracking
- No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases
